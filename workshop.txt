description=[h1]APOCALIPSE [BR] - Radio Framework[/h1]
description=Brings the dead airwaves back to life. Six unique radio channels with immersive, lore-rich transmissions — emergency alerts, survivor stories, military chatter, paranormal whispers, cryptic numbers, and a mysterious archivist who monitors them all.
description=
description=Supports English and Brazilian Portuguese. Fully configurable via sandbox options. Other modders can use this framework to add their own channels.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h1]Channels[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Emergency Broadcast System (91.6 MHz)[/b] — Evacuation orders and survival directives from what remains of Central Command.
description=[*] [b]Ghost Radio (66.6 MHz)[/b] — Unsettling whispers and cryptic warnings from a frequency no one claims to operate.
description=[*] [b]Radio Apocalipse (104.2 MHz)[/b] — The voice of the survivors. Horde warnings, supply drops, and words of hope.
description=[*] [b]Military Communications (310 kHz)[/b] — Intercepted tactical operations from soldiers fighting a war they've already lost.
description=[*] [b]Numbers Station (47.8 MHz)[/b] — Enigmatic number sequences. Its purpose is unknown. Its origin is untraceable.
description=[*] [b]The Alexandria Library (100.0 MHz)[/b] — A solitary archivist monitors every frequency. No one knows where she transmits from.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]
description=Found under [b]ApocalipseBRRadio[/b] in Sandbox Settings:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Language[/b] — English or Portugues BR
description=[*] [b]Keep Radio Alive[/b] — prevents radios from going silent after vanilla broadcasts end
description=[*] [b]Transmission Interval[/b] — minutes between broadcasts (1-60)
description=[*] [b]Toggle each channel[/b] — enable/disable any channel individually
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]

description=[h1]For Modders[/h1]
description=Add your own channels from your mod — no need to edit this mod's files.
description=
description=[b]1.[/b] Add [code]require=apocalipsebrradio[/code] to your mod.info
description=[b]2.[/b] Create a Lua file under media/lua/shared/ and require the framework:
description=[code]require "ApocalipseBRRadio/ABRRadioFramework"[/code]
description=
description=[b]3.[/b] Register a channel and add transmissions:
description=[code]
description=ABRRadio.registerChannel({
description=    id = "my_channel",
description=    name = { EN = "My Channel", PTBR = "Meu Canal" },
description=    frequency = 95000,
description=    category = "Radio",
description=    color = { r = 0.0, g = 0.8, b = 1.0 },
description=})
description=
description=ABRRadio.registerTransmission("my_channel", {
description=    id = "my_tx_01",
description=    lines = {
description=        "<bzzt>",
description=        { EN = "Hello survivors!", PTBR = "Ola sobreviventes!" },
description=        "<fzzt>",
description=    },
description=    weight = 10,
description=})
description=[/code]
